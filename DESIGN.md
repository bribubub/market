# Design Direction

## Brand
- **Product**: Shoe shop marketplace
- **Audience**: Medium to large consumer market (general shoppers)

## Personality & Mood
- Clean, user-friendly, interactive, responsive
- Professional but approachable

## Palette
- **Primary**: Blue (muted, not saturated neon)
- **Surface**: White and light neutrals
- **Text**: Dark slate for readability
- **Accent**: One deliberate blue accent at key interaction points (submit buttons, active input borders)
- Not too colorful. Eyecatching through restraint, not through many colors.

## Typography
- System sans-serif stack or a clean sans like the existing Inter. Chosen because it is neutral and readable at all sizes for an e-commerce audience that skews broad.

## Dials
- **ENERGY 2** (Balanced): Friendly and inviting, not aggressive
- **RHYTHM 1** (Uniform): Auth forms are a single-purpose screen; uniformity is intentional here
- **MOTION 2** (Scroll-reveal, transitions): Subtle transitions on focus, hover, and form state changes to make the UI feel responsive
